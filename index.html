The 6 Pillars of Health & Wellness

1. Nutrition

Fuel your body with whole, plant-predominant foods rich in nutrients.

2. Exercise

Engage in regular physical activity tailored to your body's needs.

3. Restorative Sleep

Prioritize 7-9 hours of restful sleep for cellular recovery and mental clarity.

4. Stress Management

Incorporate mindfulness, meditation, and breathwork to reduce stress.

5. Social Connection

Build supportive, positive relationships that nurture emotional health.

6. Avoiding Risky Substances

Eliminate harmful substances like tobacco and moderate alcohol consumption.
